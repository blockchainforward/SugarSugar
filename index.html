Who I Am

A dancer who loves to teach.

Judith has spent over a decade training, competing, and teaching across New York, Miami, and internationally — absorbing salsa, bachata, contemporary, and jazz at their source, then bringing that knowledge back to every class she teaches.

At Cadance Company, technique and joy aren't opposites. Every class is specific, energizing, and designed to give you real progress you can feel — not in six months, but this week.

What We Offer

Six styles. One studio.

💃
Salsa
Levels 1 – 3

Cuban and New York–style salsa — footwork, timing, partner connection, and turn patterns. Structured from first steps to advanced combinations.

🎶
Bachata
Beginner & Intermediate

Body movement, close-hold technique, and the musical phrasing that makes bachata one of the world's most emotionally resonant partner dances.

🌊
Contemporary
Open Level

Fluid, expressive movement combining ballet, modern, and improvisation. Builds full-body strength and a deeper relationship with space and music.

✨
Jazz
All Levels

Classic and contemporary jazz technique — isolations, syncopation, stage presence. Fast-paced, musical, and endlessly satisfying to get right.

🎤
Hip Hop
Beginner Friendly

Street style foundations — groove, isolation, and freestyling. Classes focus on musicality and self-expression over memorized choreography.

🎯
Private Lessons
Tailored to You

One-on-one or small group sessions built entirely around your goals — competition prep, wedding choreography, or fast-tracking your progress.
