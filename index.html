The Instructor

Trained in rhythm,
rooted in passion.

Photo of Judith Add your photo here

Judith has spent over a decade refining her craft across studios in New York, Miami, and internationally — absorbing salsa, bachata, contemporary, and jazz traditions, then bringing them back to students who are ready to discover what their bodies can do.

At Cadance Company, every class is a conversation between technique and joy. Whether you're stepping onto a dance floor for the first time or preparing to compete, Judith meets you exactly where you are — and takes you further.

What We Offer

Find your style.

💃
Salsa
All Levels · Levels 1–3

Cuban and New York–style salsa rooted in rhythm, footwork, and partner connection. From first steps to complex turn patterns.

🎶
Bachata
Beginner & Intermediate

Sensual footwork, body rolls, and the close-hold connection that makes bachata one of the world's most expressive partner dances.

🌊
Contemporary
Intermediate · Open

Fluid technique blending ballet, modern, and improvisation — building strength, spatial awareness, and artistic confidence.

✨
Jazz
All Levels

Classic and theatrical jazz technique — isolations, syncopation, big stage presence. Fun, fast-paced, and full of personality.

🎤
Hip Hop
Beginner Friendly

Foundations of street style, breaking, and freestyling. Classes focus on musicality, groove, and self-expression.

🎯
Private Instruction
Tailored to You

One-on-one or small group sessions built around your goals — competition prep, wedding choreography, or simply accelerating your progress.
