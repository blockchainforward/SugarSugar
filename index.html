Who I Am

A dancer who loves to teach.

Judith Marie is a Salsa On2 specialist — trained in body mechanics, musicality, and the partner dynamics that make On2 one of the most expressive forms of social dance in the world. Her teaching breaks down complex movement into things you can actually feel and apply.

At Cadance Company, technique and joy aren't opposites. Every class is specific, energizing, and designed to give you real progress you can feel — not in six months, but this week.

Common Questions

Answers before you ask.

Do I need dance experience to join?+

No experience needed. Both Body Movement and Pasos & Estilo are open to all levels — Judith teaches from first principles, so beginners get a real foundation and experienced dancers get technique they've been missing.

What does "On2" mean?+

Salsa On2 (also called New York style or Mambo) means the main break step lands on beat 2 of the music, rather than beat 1. It syncs more closely with the clave rhythm and conga patterns in the music, which gives the dance a distinct feel — smoother, more musical, and deeply connected to how the instruments phrase. If you've only danced On1 before, you'll notice the difference immediately.

What's the difference between Body Movement and Pasos & Estilo?+

Body Movement is about how you use your body — isolations, ribcage movement, hip action, and musicality. You'll work on connecting each part of your body to the rhythm. Pasos & Estilo focuses on footwork patterns, turn sequences, and individual styling like arms and hand lines. They're designed to complement each other — many students take both the same night.

How does the online live class work?+

You join the class via a live video link at the same time as the in-person class. Judith teaches to both groups simultaneously and gives feedback on camera when she can. You'll need enough space to move freely, something to watch on (laptop, tablet, or TV), and a decent internet connection. The link is sent after you reserve via WhatsApp.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes you can move in. For footwear, anything with a smooth sole works — avoid thick rubber soles like sneakers, which grip the floor and make turning difficult. Dance shoes are great if you have them; leather-soled shoes or socks on a smooth floor work too. Don't overthink it for your first class.

How do I reserve my spot?+

Message Judith via WhatsApp at +1 801 388 4572. Tell her which class (Body Movement, Pasos & Estilo, or both) and whether you're joining in person or online. She'll confirm your spot and send you any details you need.

Can I join if classes have already started?+

Yes. Both classes are designed so you can drop in at any point — Judith structures each session to be accessible whether it's your first class or your twentieth. Just message to reserve your spot before coming.

Are private lessons available online?+

Yes, private lessons are available online via video call. For technique-focused work, online sessions are just as effective as in person. For choreography or partner work, in-person is usually preferred — but Judith can discuss what format makes the most sense for your goals.
