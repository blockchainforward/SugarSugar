The Beginning

BORN FROM
RHYTHM.

My story began across continents — Germany, Greece, Portugal, Latin America — where dance became part of who I am.

I started ballet and jazz at age five. Years of training led me to complete my exams with the Royal Academy of Dance in London, and later to graduate in contemporary and modern dance from Danceworks Berlin in 2017.

From there, the world opened. I trained in salsa, bachata, zouk, and Argentine tango across Argentina, Brazil, and Cuba — going deep into each style's roots, not just its surface steps.

In 2024, I became World Vice Champion in On2 at the World Salsa Summit in Miami. In 2025, I toured Europe and Asia alongside Marco Ferrigno of Ansima.

"Dance was never just something I did. It became the way I express, connect, and inspire."
